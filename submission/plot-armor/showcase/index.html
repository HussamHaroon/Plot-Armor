[ 03 · AI/ML ]

Noisy Labels: HelpDeskNet-50K

Why does a 50,000-ticket classifier plateau at 82%? Because the labels are noisy — and we quantified it: 15.0% crowd error (Wilson 95% CI [11.8%, 18.8%]), a real Tier A/B quality gap (z = 4.22, p ≈ 2.4×10⁻⁵), systematic Billing↔Refund confusion (43% of errors), and a true-accuracy ceiling of ~96% under uniform-flip assumptions.

A simulation reproduces the 82%/96% split empirically, and the expert-review experiment shows combined scoring achieves 98.5% review yield — 6.4× more error fixed per expert-hour than random selection.
